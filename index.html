NYU WIRELESS Research

Next-generation wireless, from theory and circuits to testbeds and applications

NYU WIRELESS is an academic-industry research center at the NYU Tandon School of Engineering. Our faculty and students helped pioneer the millimeter-wave research behind 5G, and today work across the upper mid-band, sub-THz, AI-native networks, and the circuits, testbeds and applications that bring them to life.
