NYU WIRELESS was proud to host the
[Brooklyn AI-Native 6G Summit](https://www.b6gs.com/) at the NYU Tandon
School of Engineering on November 3&ndash;5, 2026. This was the 13th edition
of the summit, co-hosted with our long-time partner Nokia. This year's theme
was *Networks at the dawn of the AI Supercycle*.

More than 300 people attended in person, and many more watched the live stream
on IEEE TV. Speakers from industry and academia gave keynotes and joined
panels, and nearly 50 live demos showed current research. The summit opened
on November 3 with the
[NYU WIRELESS Open House]({{ '/events/open-house-2026/' | relative_url }}).

## Why "AI-Native"

This year, "AI-Native" was added to the summit's name. Wireless connectivity
and artificial intelligence are quickly becoming inseparable. Wireless is what
makes intelligence ubiquitous. It moves AI beyond data centers and into
smartphones, autonomous vehicles, microchips and embedded systems. At the same
time, AI is reshaping wireless itself, in AI-RAN, chip design, software and
physical-layer performance.

> What makes this summit truly unique is the powerful intersection of industry
> and academia. As researchers, having these candid conversations with the
> industry leaders who build and deploy next-generation networks ensures that
> our work addresses real-world challenges and delivers tangible impact.
>
> &mdash; Sundeep Rangan, Director, NYU WIRELESS

## Thank you

We are deeply grateful to Nokia. Nokia was a founding member of NYU WIRELESS,
and our collaboration goes back to Bell Labs. It runs from early work on
millimeter-wave for 5G to today's joint research on the upper mid-band,
satellite systems and AI-native architectures. A special thanks to Amitava
Ghosh of Nokia, the intellectual and operational lead of the summit, who
designed the entire program and brought together its diverse set of speakers.

Thank you also to all of our speakers and attendees, and to the faculty
members and students whose hard work made the summit possible.
