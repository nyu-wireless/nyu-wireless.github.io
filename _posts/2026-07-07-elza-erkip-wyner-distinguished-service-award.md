NYU WIRELESS faculty member **Elza Erkip** has received the 2026
[Aaron D. Wyner Distinguished Service Award](https://www.itsoc.org/honors/wyner-award).
She was announced as the recipient during the ISIT 2026 Award Ceremony.

The award honors an individual who has shown outstanding leadership in, and
provided long-standing, exceptional service to, the information theory
community.

## About Elza Erkip

Elza Erkip is an Institute Professor in the Electrical and Computer
Engineering Department at the NYU Tandon School of Engineering. Her research
interests are in information theory, communication theory and wireless
communications.

She received the B.S. degree in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from
Middle East Technical University in Ankara, Turkey, and the M.S. and Ph.D.
degrees in Electrical Engineering from Stanford University.

A list of her Information Theory Society awards and service is on her
[Information Theory Society profile](https://www.itsoc.org/profile/8892).
