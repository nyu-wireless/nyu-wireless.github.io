The [Wirelessnerd.org](https://wirelessnerd.org/) website is now ready for
your queries.

## Getting access

To use the 3GPP part of the site, you will receive a one-time code by email at
your corporate email account.

## Questions to try

Here are some examples of 3GPP questions you can ask:

- What is the radar cross section (RCS) modeling equation in recent 3GPP
  documents?
- What does TR 38.901 say?
- What were the recent contributions of RAN1?
- What is the precoder design for the Rel 18 Doppler?
